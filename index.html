About the property

Description property

Property Partners Moore Larkin, together with joint selling agent Property Partners Richard Cleary are delighted to offer for sale this superb three bedroom residence. Centrally located on the Old Dublin Road, Cnoc Mhuire is just a ten minute walk from Athlone town centre and also a ten minute walk in the opposite direction to TUS Campus Athlone. The property consists of a three bedroom, detached bungalow with a detached domestic garage adjacent. The full living accommodation which measures a total area of approximately 93sq.m / 1,001 contains the following; Sitting room, kitchen / dining room, three bedrooms and family bathroom. This property is ideally suited to prospective buyers seeking a bungalow style home close to Athlone town centre and it will also appeal to investors given its prime central location and not having been previously rented.

Viewings are highly recommended.
